# 🚀 Asteroids Game (Java)

This project is a **2D Asteroids game** created from scratch in **Java**. In this game, you control a spaceship that must destroy asteroids flying at it to survive. The game includes multiple levels, increasing difficulty, and engaging AI bots for added challenge.

  ![Demo](https://github.com/drewberry612/asteroids/blob/main/asteroidsA.gif)

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Features

- **Multiple Levels**: The game includes progressively harder levels with **random backgrounds** and **stage items**. The player must destroy all asteroids in each level to progress to the next stage.
  
- **Asteroid Mechanics**: When asteroids are destroyed, they break into **multiple fragments** that fly in different directions, making it harder to survive and adding unpredictability to the gameplay.

- **Boundless World**: The game world is **boundless**, meaning if you fly off one side of the screen, your spaceship is relocated to the opposite side. This simulates flying around a large space environment.

- **Power-ups**: Destroyed asteroids sometimes drop **power-ups** that enhance the player's abilities, such as faster shooting or temporary invincibility. These power-ups are essential for surviving more challenging levels.

---

## 🤖 AI Bots

- **Enemy Spaceships**: Basic **AI-controlled spaceships** are included, each with unique behaviors, such as:
  - Flying directly at the player.
  - Trying to avoid the player at all costs.
  
- These AI bots add an extra layer of challenge, making the game more engaging.

---

## ⚡ Gameplay Enhancements

- **Particle Effects**: When asteroids or enemy ships are destroyed, **particle effects** are triggered to enhance the visual experience.
  
- **Dynamic Backgrounds**: Randomly chosen background elements provide a unique environment for each level.

- **Sound Effects**: The game includes various **sound effects** for shooting, explosions, and player interactions to make the experience more immersive.

- **Two Difficulty Levels**: The game has two difficulty settings:
  - **Normal**: Standard gameplay.
  - **Easy**: Includes a shield that recharges after a few seconds of not taking damage, providing an easier experience for beginners.
